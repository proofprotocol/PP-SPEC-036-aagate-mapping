# Contributing

PP-SPEC-036 is maintained as part of the Proof Protocol specification series.

Contributions may be submitted as issues or pull requests.

Contributions should preserve Proof Protocol's framework-agnostic architecture, distinguish upstream AAGATE concepts from Proof Protocol concepts, avoid unnecessary reproduction of upstream material, identify sources clearly, and preserve the normative meaning of this specification.

By contributing, you represent that you have the right to submit the contribution.
